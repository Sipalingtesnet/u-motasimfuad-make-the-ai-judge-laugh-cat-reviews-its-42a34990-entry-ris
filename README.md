**Line:** "One star. Left him a warm mouse on the rug, plated the way he plates dinner. He screamed and fetched the broom. I have never once screamed at the cold dead chicken he buys in a bag."

A house cat files a one-star review of its human after a carefully presented gift is refused, and holds the human to the same table manners the human expects of the cat.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic, offline, no secrets, no arguments; `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` redirects the output directory.
